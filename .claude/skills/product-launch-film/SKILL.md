# Product Launch Film

## Inputs
Use the approved brief, product screenshots/captures, brand assets, copy, narration, music, and usage rights. If product UI is supplied, do not fabricate UI states or features.

## Story structure
Choose only the beats that serve the brief:
1. Hook / tension
2. Product or idea reveal
3. Proof through a real feature, workflow, or visual demonstration
4. Benefits stated only when supported by supplied facts
5. Brand lockup and CTA

## Execution
- Create a storyboard and duration budget first.
- Use Remotion for frame-accurate React scenes unless the brief explicitly selects HTML/HyperFrames.
- Build reusable scenes for title, product reveal, feature proof, quote (only approved), and CTA.
- Prefer authentic product captures over fake screenshots.
- If narration exists, align scene changes to the actual transcript and timings.
- Design distinct 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 compositions rather than relying on center cropping.

## Final QA
Check factual accuracy, text legibility, pacing, brand consistency, audio levels, ending hold, and correct export dimensions. State any unverified claims or missing assets.
