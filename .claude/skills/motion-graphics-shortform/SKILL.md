# Motion Graphics & Short-form Video

- Define one idea per beat and a clear hook near the beginning.
- Prefer visual evidence, original vector shapes, and approved product captures over generic stock imagery.
- Keep captions and key subjects inside platform-safe areas.
- Use a clear rhythm of contrast, reveal, hold, and transition; do not make every shot equally busy.
- For vertical, square, and landscape deliverables, recompose typography and subject placement per aspect ratio.
- Generate B-roll from a text brief only when the source facts and desired visual style are clear.
- Do not imply generated B-roll is real product footage.
- Keep the CTA readable and leave enough time for the viewer to process it.
- Deliver exact dimensions, duration, frame rate, codec, and a list of assets used.
